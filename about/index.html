ASF Electricon is a family-owned electrical contractor, built in South Florida and trusted by the contractors who power its network.

Our Story

Engineering roots. Relationship-driven.

Our founder, Tony Figueroa, is an electrical engineer who spent years at FPL before starting his own company.

Born in Cuba and raised in Puerto Rico, Tony built the business on technical know-how. When he says a job can or cannot be done, he is almost always right, and on the most complicated relocations, he is still the one we send.

His daughter, Alexis Silverio, joined him nearly five years ago and became the face of the company. Born and raised in Miami, she built the relationships that took ASF from a handful of jobs to more than two hundred, and today she owns and leads the company.

“I show up, I’m friendly, and I own it when I make a mistake. This is on me, and I’ll fix it. That’s really all it is.”

Alexis Silverio, Owner
